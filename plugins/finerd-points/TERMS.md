# finerd points — Terms of Use

> Draft under legal review. The final text is published here before the first release.
> Summary of the draft: finerd points is open-source skills for AI assistants, not a service.
> The skills have no server and send your data nowhere; your ledger lives in a folder on your own
> computer. Your AI assistant reads the sites you open and sign in to yourself, in your own browser;
> following each site's terms is between you, your AI platform and the site, and some banks restrict
> AI tools. finerd points never handles passwords or codes and never acts on a site. Community
> contributions, including site-specific short paths (GitHub version only, under the contributor's
> responsibility), are provided as is under the MIT License.
